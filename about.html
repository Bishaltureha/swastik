Our story

A tradition, packed with care.

Since 1992, Swastik has believed that every home deserves pure poha at an honest price.

Poha is not just breakfast.

For generations, poha has been the dish that welcomes the morning in Indian homes — quick to make, gentle on the stomach, loved by every age.

In 1992 Swastik began with a simple belief: clean, good-quality poha should be within everyone’s reach. We chose the Swastik, an ancient symbol of good fortune and well-being, as our mark — because that is what we hope every pack brings to your home.

Today, Swastik Premium Poha is manufactured by Shree Sai Hasti Agro Products Limited in Navsari, Gujarat, and marketed by Aarem Food Processing LLP, Surat — with a fresh new look and the same trusted taste.
