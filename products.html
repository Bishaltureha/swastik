Usage guide

The perfect Kanda Poha.

The classic Indian breakfast, ready in about 20 minutes.

Serves
2
Total time
About 20 minutes
Swastik Poha
1 cup (about 100 g)

You’ll also need

2 tbsp oil1 tsp mustard seeds8–10 curry leaves1–2 green chillies2 tbsp peanuts1 onion, chopped½ tsp turmeric1 tsp sugarSalt to taste½ lemonCoriander, coconut & sev
  1. 01

    Rinse the poha

    Place the poha in a colander and rinse under running water for 20–30 seconds, tossing gently, until the flakes turn soft but still hold their shape. Never soak them.

    1 min
  2. 02

    Rest & season

    Drain well. Sprinkle salt, sugar and turmeric, mix lightly with a fork and rest for 5 minutes so the flakes finish softening on their own.

    5 min
  3. 03

    Temper the oil

    Heat oil in a kadhai. Add mustard seeds and let them crackle, then add peanuts, curry leaves and green chillies. Fry until the peanuts turn crunchy.

    2 min
  4. 04

    Sauté the onion

    Add chopped onion and cook until translucent. For a heartier plate, cook diced potato first until tender, then add the onion.

    4 min
  5. 05

    Combine gently

    Add the poha and fold carefully on a low flame for 2–3 minutes so every flake is coated. Cover and steam for 2 minutes.

    5 min
  6. 06

    Finish & serve

    Squeeze fresh lemon over the top. Garnish with coriander, grated coconut and sev. Serve hot with a cup of masala chai.

    Serve hot

Cook’s tip: rinse, never soak · use a wide pan · keep the flame low once the poha goes in · fold, don’t stir.
