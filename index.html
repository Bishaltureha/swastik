Part of our everyday traditions

Some foods are more than just meals — they're part of our everyday traditions. Poha is one such favourite, bringing warmth and flavour to breakfast tables across India.

Swastik Premium Poha brings this timeless favourite to your kitchen, ready to be enjoyed in your own delicious way.

Simple

Just one ingredient: flattened rice (paddy poha).

Tasty

Warm, comforting flavour for every breakfast table.

Versatile

Make it your way, from classic to creative.

Quick & Easy

Breakfast in four simple steps

Rinse

Rinse the poha gently in a strainer and let it soften.

Temper

Heat oil, add mustard seeds, curry leaves, green chilli and onion.

Mix

Add turmeric and salt, then toss in the poha and warm through.

Serve

Finish with lemon and coriander. Enjoy it hot.
